Earlier negative results and training repairs

The earlier compact-vocabulary pilot was faster per step but scored worse at both tested budgets. Its 800-step extension was exploratory. The original vocabulary stays available and remains the vocabulary default; the new architecture is the default for new runs based on the paired study, while continued models inherit their own architecture.

Hard phrase blocking eliminated repeated phrases mechanically but distorted speaker labels. Unlikelihood reduced token repetition in the continued Shakespeare trial, yet word repetition worsened relative to ordinary continuation. Standard likelihood and unpenalized sampling therefore remain the defaults.

The new worker also saves best weights immediately, restores optimizer/random state on resume, excludes bias/norm vectors from weight decay, and retains raw uploads. Continued training uses a private run-local copy of the source checkpoint. Originals remain read-only.

Research and reproducibility

The architecture papers motivate experiments; their large-model results do not establish gains for VV-GPT. The unlikelihood objective is a bounded 32-token context adaptation, not a full paper reproduction. Protocol, configurations, hashes, measurements and all public samples are retained locally in the project.
